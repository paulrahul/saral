Artificial intelligence tools are computer helpers. You can ask them to write, explain, translate, or suggest ideas.

## Free tools to try

- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) — Ask questions, write messages, or explain difficult words.
- [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) — Ask questions and get help with learning or planning.
- [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com/) — Ask for writing help, ideas, or simple explanations.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) — Make simple posters, cards, and pictures. Some features are free.

## How to use one

1. Open the tool’s website.
2. Type what you need in the message box.
3. Use simple words, such as:  
   **“Explain this letter in easy English.”**
4. Read the answer carefully.
5. Ask again if you do not understand.

## Important safety tips

- Do not share your password, bank details, or private identity number.
- Do not believe every answer. The tool can make mistakes.
- Check important information with a trusted person or website.
- Free tools may offer paid features. Do not enter payment details unless you want to pay.
- Be careful when downloading files or clicking links.

## Useful things to ask

- “Write a polite message to my landlord.”
- “Translate this into simple English.”
- “Give me a shopping list for five cheap meals.”
- “Explain this health letter in easy words.”
- “Help me practise a job interview.”
