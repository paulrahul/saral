[Artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence), or **AI**, is a computer helper. It can look at a document and explain it in simple words.

## What can it do?

An AI tool may help you:

- Read a letter or notice
- Make a long document shorter
- Explain difficult words
- Find a name, date, price, or address
- Translate a document
- Answer questions about the document

## How to use it

1. Open an AI app or website.
2. Choose **Add file**, **Upload**, or the camera picture button.
3. Take a clear picture of the document, or choose a saved file.
4. Ask a simple question, such as:
   - “Please explain this in simple words.”
   - “What date must I remember?”
   - “How much money does this say?”
5. Read the answer carefully.

## Stay safe

- Do not upload passwords, bank details, identity numbers, or private medical papers unless you trust the service.
- Check important information with the original document.
- AI can make mistakes. Ask a trusted person or office if something is unclear.
- Make sure the picture shows the whole page and is not blurry.
