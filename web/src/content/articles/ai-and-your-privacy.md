## What is AI?
AI ([Artificial Intelligence](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)) is like a very smart robot inside your computer or phone. It learns by looking at lots of information. When you ask it a question, it uses what it has learned to give you an answer. 

## How Does AI See My Information?
When you type a question to an AI program, it reads what you wrote. Some companies that make these AI programs might save what you type so the robot can learn even more for the next person. 

## Why Does This Matter for My Privacy?
Privacy means keeping your personal life a secret from strangers. If you tell the AI your bank password, your home address, or a secret about your family, that information might be saved by the company. We do not want our secrets to be saved where strangers might see them.

## How to Keep Your Secrets Safe
1. **Do not share passwords:** Never tell an AI program your passwords.
2. **Do not share bank details:** Keep your money safe by not typing your card numbers into AI tools.
3. **Do not share personal secrets:** If you wouldn't tell a stranger on the street, do not tell the AI.

## The Golden Rule
Think of AI like a helpful stranger. You can ask it to help you write a letter or find a recipe, but never share your private information with it.
