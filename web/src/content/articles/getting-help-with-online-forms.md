Sometimes, you need to fill out a form on the internet. This is just like a paper form you fill out with a pen, but you type on your computer or phone screen. Online forms are used for things like making a doctor's appointment, asking for government help, or buying something.

If you get stuck while looking at a form on a screen, do not worry. Here is how you can get help:

### Ask Someone You Trust
The best way to get help is to ask a family member, a good friend, or a neighbor you trust. You can also ask a worker at your local library or community center. They can sit next to you and point to the screen to show you what to do.

### Look for Help on the Website
Most websites have a way to get help built right in. Look around the edges of the screen for words like:
*   **Help**
*   **FAQ** (which stands for "Frequently Asked Questions")
*   **Contact Us**

Clicking on these words will usually show you a phone number to call or a message box where you can type your problem to the website owners.

### Be Safe When Asking for Help
Only share your personal details (like your bank numbers, date of birth, or passwords) with people you know and trust, or with the official website itself. 

**Never** give your personal information to someone who calls you on the phone out of nowhere and says they will help you fill out a form. Bad people sometimes pretend to be helpers to steal your money. Always go to the website yourself or ask a family member to help you find it.
