AI (Artificial Intelligence) is like a very smart computer program that can talk to you, write things, or make pictures. You might use it on your phone or computer to ask questions or get help.

While it is a helpful tool, we must be careful, just like when we cross the street or talk to strangers.

Here is how you can stay safe:

### 1. Keep Your Secrets Safe

Never tell the computer helper your private information. This includes your bank account numbers, passwords, home address, or phone number. Think of the computer helper like a stranger on the bus. You wouldn't tell a stranger your secret money hiding spot!

### 2. Computers Can Make Mistakes

AI is smart, but it does not know everything. Sometimes, it might tell you something that is not true. If you need important advice about your health or your money, always ask a real doctor or a trusted family member. Do not trust the computer completely.

### 3. Watch Out for Fake Voices and Videos

Bad people can use AI to copy the voice of someone you know, like your grandchild. They might call you on the phone and ask for money, sounding just like them. If someone calls asking for money in a strange way, hang up and call them back on a phone number you know is real.

### 4. Be the Boss

You are in charge. If the computer helper says something that feels wrong or makes you uncomfortable, you can stop using it or ask it to change the subject. You do not have to do what it says. 

By following these simple steps, you can enjoy using new computer helpers without worry!
