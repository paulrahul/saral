Artificial intelligence tools can help answer questions, write messages, make pictures, and more.

## Some tools are free

You may use some tools without paying money. But they may still need:

- An internet connection
- A phone, tablet, or computer
- Electricity to charge your device
- Your email address to make an account

“Free” can also mean the company may show you adverts or collect information about how you use the tool.

## Some tools need payment

Some tools have a monthly or yearly price. Others charge each time you use a special feature, such as:

- Making many pictures
- Changing voices
- Reading long documents
- Using a faster service
- Removing adverts

Always check the price before starting a free trial. A free trial may begin charging you later.

## You may pay with your information

Some tools ask for your name, email address, photos, or questions. Read carefully before sharing private information.

Do not share:

- Passwords
- Bank details
- Identity numbers
- Private medical information
- Someone else’s personal information

## Check before spending money

Ask yourself:

1. Do I really need this tool?
2. Is there a free choice?
3. What will I pay each month?
4. Will payment start automatically?
5. Can I cancel easily?

Use a trusted adult or family member for help if you are unsure. Never give payment details to a website you do not trust.
