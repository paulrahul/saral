## What is AI?

[AI](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) stands for "Artificial Intelligence." It is a computer program that can answer questions, write text, or make pictures. You might have heard of tools like ChatGPT or Google Gemini. These are AI tools.

## Why Do I Need to Check AI Answers?

AI is very smart, but it is **not perfect**. Sometimes AI:

- Makes mistakes
- Gives wrong information
- Makes up things that are not true
- Does not know the latest news

Think of AI like a person you just met. You would not believe everything they say without checking, right? The same is true for AI.

## Simple Ways to Check AI Answers

### 1. Ask Google

If AI gives you a fact or information, type the same question into [Google](https://www.google.com). See if other websites say the same thing.

**Example:** If AI tells you "The tallest mountain is Mount Everest," search "tallest mountain in the world" on Google to check.

### 2. Look at More Than One Source

Do not trust just one website. Check **two or three** different websites. If they all say the same thing, the information is probably correct.

### 3. Check the Date

Sometimes AI gives old information. Look for the date on the website you are checking. Make sure the information is **recent**.

### 4. Ask the Same Question Differently

Ask AI the same question in a different way. If the answer changes too much, be careful. The information might not be reliable.

**Example:**
- First question: "What is the population of India?"
- Second question: "How many people live in India?"

If the answers are very different, check on Google.

### 5. Use Common Sense

Ask yourself: "Does this answer make sense?" If something sounds too good to be true, it might be wrong.

**Example:** If AI tells you "Drinking lemon water cures all diseases," your common sense should tell you this is not true.

## When to Be Extra Careful

Always check AI answers carefully when the information is about:

- **Health or medicine** - Talk to a doctor
- **Money or investments** - Talk to a bank officer or financial expert
- **Legal matters** - Talk to a lawyer
- **Important decisions** - Talk to someone you trust

AI is a **helper**, not an expert. For important things, always ask a real person who knows the subject.
