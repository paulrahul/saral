## What is AI?

**AI** stands for **Artificial Intelligence**. It means computer programs that can understand questions and give answers, almost like talking to a person. You can learn more about it here: [Artificial Intelligence - Simple Wikipedia](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence).

Think of AI like a very smart helper who knows about many things and can talk to you.

## How is it Different from Google?

When you use **Google** to search, you get a list of websites. You must open them and read to find your answer.

With AI, you ask a question in plain words, and the AI **writes you an answer directly**. You do not need to open many websites.

## Examples of AI Helpers

Some popular AI helpers are:

- **Google Assistant** on your phone
- **Siri** on Apple phones
- **ChatGPT** on the internet

These tools are mostly **free** to use.

## How to Ask AI a Question

1. Open the AI app or website.
2. Type your question in the box, or speak if it has a voice option.
3. Press send or enter.
4. Read the answer it gives you.

## What You Can Ask

You can ask AI many things, like:

- "How do I cook rice?"
- "What is the weather today?"
- "How do I send a photo on WhatsApp?"
- "What buses go to the hospital?"

## Important Things to Remember

- AI is helpful, but it can sometimes be **wrong**. Always check important information.
- Do not share personal secrets, like passwords or bank details, with AI.
- AI is a tool to help you, but you are still in charge.

## Summary

AI is like a smart friend on your phone or computer. You ask, and it answers. It can save you time and help you learn new things every day.
