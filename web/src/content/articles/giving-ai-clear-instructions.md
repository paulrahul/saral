[Artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) is a computer helper that follows your instructions.

Clear instructions help it understand what you want.

## A simple way to ask

Tell it:

1. **What you want**
2. **Who it is for**
3. **How you want the answer**
4. **Any important details**

## Example

Instead of saying:

> Write a letter.

Say:

> Write a short, polite letter to my landlord. Tell them that the kitchen tap is leaking. Use simple words.

The second instruction gives more helpful details.

## You can also give an example

> Write a shopping list like this:  
> Bread, milk, rice, and apples.  
> Make a list for a family of four.

## Ask it to change the answer

You can say:

- “Make it shorter.”
- “Use easier words.”
- “Explain it step by step.”
- “Give me three examples.”
- “Translate it into Hindi.”
- “Read it like a friendly teacher.”

## Remember

The computer may sometimes make mistakes. Do not share private information such as:

- Passwords
- Bank details
- Identity card numbers
- Private medical information

Check important answers with a trusted person or a reliable source.
