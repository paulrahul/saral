You may hear people talk about Artificial Intelligence, or [AI](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence). AI is a computer program that can seem to think a little like a person. But how does it learn?

## 1. AI Is Like a Student

Imagine a child learning what a cat is. You show them many pictures of cats. You point and say, "This is a cat." After seeing many examples, the child learns to spot a cat on their own.

AI learns in a very similar way. We do not tell it every rule. We show it many examples.

## 2. What Is Data?

[Data](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data) is just collected information. It can be:
- Pictures
- Words
- Numbers
- Sounds

If we want an AI to know cats, we give it thousands of cat pictures. The pictures are the data.

## 3. Finding Patterns

The AI looks at all the examples. It looks for patterns. For cats, it might notice:
- Pointy ears
- Whiskers
- A tail

It remembers these patterns. This process is called [machine learning](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning).

## 4. Practice Makes Better

The AI practises again and again. It tries to guess, "Is this a cat?" If it is wrong, it adjusts its thinking. Over time, it gets better, just like a person practising a new skill.

## 5. Then It Can Help Us

After learning, the AI can help us. It can:
- Sort our photos
- Help a doctor spot illness in an X-ray
- Suggest a book we might like

## Important to Remember

AI is not magic. It is a tool that learns from the information we give it. If the information is bad or unfair, the AI can make mistakes. That is why people must watch over it and use it carefully.
