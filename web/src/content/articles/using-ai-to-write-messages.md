Artificial intelligence can help you write a message. It is like a writing helper on your phone or computer.

## How to use it

1. Open an artificial intelligence writing tool.
2. Tell it what you want to say.
3. Say who the message is for and what feeling you want.

For example:

> Write a short, friendly message to my doctor. Say that I cannot come to my appointment tomorrow and ask for a new date.

The tool may write:

> Hello Doctor, I cannot come to my appointment tomorrow. Could I please have a new date? Thank you.

## Check the message

Before sending:

- Read the whole message.
- Make sure the details are correct.
- Change any words that do not sound like you.
- Check the person’s name and date.
- Send it only when you are happy with it.

## Keep private things safe

Do not type private information into a writing tool, such as:

- Passwords
- Bank details
- Identity numbers
- Private medical information

Artificial intelligence can make mistakes. You are the person who must check the message before sending it.
