# Roadmap

Priorities run from P1 (highest) to P3.

## Features

- P1: Video content, as video articles or as articles with embedded video
- P1: Dynamic article layout and style based on the article's content (all articles currently share one layout)
- P1: AI help bot in each article
- P1: User login
  - P1: Basic profile management
  - P1: Like button for articles
  - P1: Topic preferences
  - P1: Read history
  - P3: Feedback on articles
- P2: Contextual home page content
  - Based on like counts
  - Based on unread articles
- P2: Newsletter
- P2: Comments
- P3: A way for readers to suggest new topics

## Bugs and reliability

- P1: Article summaries are sometimes truncated
- P2: Images sometimes contain garbage characters and words
